Delete your Shamble account

Last updated: 23 September 2026

This page explains how to request deletion of your Shamble account and the data associated with it. Shamble is published by Samuel Samson.

How to request deletion

  1. Email samsonsamuel531@gmail.com from any address.
  2. Put Delete my Shamble account in the subject line.
  3. In the message, tell us the email address you signed in with — whether you signed in directly or via Google.

You do not need an account on any other website, and there is nothing to fill in. A single email is enough.

We reply to confirm the request, then delete the account. Requests are completed within 30 days.

What is deleted

DataWhat happens
Account identifier and email addressDeleted permanently

What is kept, and for how long

  • Purchase records. Google Play and RevenueCat keep records of in-app purchases for billing, tax, and refund obligations. We cannot delete these, and they are not covered by this request. They are held under Google's Privacy Policy and RevenueCat's Privacy Policy.
  • Server backups. Deleted records may persist in encrypted database backups for up to 30 days after deletion, after which the backups expire and the data is gone.

Data that never reaches us

Your run history, in-game currency balance, and settings are stored only on your phone. Uninstalling Shamble removes them. They are not part of a deletion request because we never receive them. Your live location while playing is used on-device and to fetch map tiles, and is likewise not stored on our servers — see the Privacy Policy for details.
