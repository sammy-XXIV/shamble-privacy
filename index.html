Privacy Policy for Shamble

Last updated: 23 September 2026

Shamble is a GPS zombie-chase fitness game: you walk or jog outdoors and the game tracks your real position to place zombies around you on a live map. This policy explains exactly what data that requires, why, and who it is shared with. It is written to match what the app actually does — see also the Terms of Service.

What is collected

Precise location. Shamble requests continuous, high-accuracy GPS location while you are playing a run. This is the core mechanic — without it the game cannot place zombies relative to where you are or track distance covered. Your live position is used to render the map and drive the chase on your device. It is also sent, as map tile requests, to our map providers (see below) so the streets around you can be drawn.

Account identifier and email. You can sign in with an email and password or with Google. Either way an account is created via Firebase Authentication so your progress belongs to you across sessions and devices. If you sign in with Google, we receive the account identifier and email address associated with that Google account.

Purchases. If you buy an in-game pack (ammo, escape charges, revives), the purchase is handled by Google Play and processed through RevenueCat, our subscription/purchase management provider. RevenueCat receives your Google Play purchase token and an anonymous app-user identifier so the item can be granted and restored on future devices. We never see or store your payment card or billing details — those stay with Google Play.

What stays on your device

Your run history, in-game currency balance, and settings are stored locally on your phone and are not transmitted to us. Uninstalling the game deletes them.

What is not collected

  • No contacts, photos, files, camera, or microphone access
  • No advertising identifiers, and no ads
  • No third-party analytics or behavioural tracking
  • No crash or diagnostic reporting sent off-device

Who the data is shared with

  • Firebase (Google) — handles account creation and sign-in, and stores your account identifier and email address.
  • Google Play — processes sign-in and billing for any purchases.
  • RevenueCat — processes and validates purchases on our behalf. Receives your purchase token and an anonymous app-user ID, not your email or payment details.
  • Mapbox and TomTom — while you play, the game requests map tiles for the area around your current position so streets can be drawn on screen. These requests include the map tile coordinates for your general area (not your exact GPS coordinates) and your device's IP address, which these providers may use as described in their own privacy policies (Mapbox, TomTom).

Data is not sold, and is not shared with anyone beyond the providers listed above.

How long it is kept

Your account and its associated email are kept while your account exists. Ask us to delete them and they will be removed.

Your choices

  • Location access can be denied or revoked at any time in your device's app permissions — the game simply cannot run a live chase without it.
  • Sign out at any time from the Settings screen.
  • Email us to have your account and associated data deleted.

Children

Shamble is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has provided information, contact us and it will be deleted.
